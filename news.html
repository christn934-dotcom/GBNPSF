02 School news

Written by the office and by Class 6 reporters on the school newsletter. Republished here once it has gone home in print.

Academics

14 November 2026 3 min read

Reading week returns with a bilingual book swap

Every class is paired with a reading buddy from another year, and the library is taking donations of French and English storybooks until 30 November. Last year's swap moved 900 books; this year the target is a thousand, with a shelf reserved for Bamoun folktales in French translation.

Pupils who finish five books in the week add a leaf to the reading tree in the courtyard. The tree will be planted for real in March.

Mrs Nkeng, head teacher
Campus

6 November 2026 2 min read

New science room opens above the library

Built over the long holiday, the room seats thirty pupils and holds a fume hood, running water and locked storage for the physics and chemistry kits. Classes 5 and 6 now have two practical lessons a week instead of demonstrations at the front of a classroom.

The project was funded by school fees and a contribution from the parents' association. A small plaque by the door names the families who gave materials.

24 October 2026 4 min read

Ninety-eight per cent of Class 6 placed in secondary

Forty-one pupils sat the common entrance examination this year; forty were placed in a lycée or college of their choice. Two received scholarships — one to a school in Yaoundé, one in Bafoussam — and both are the first in their families to attend secondary school outside the West Region.

The school's graduation ceremony was held in the courtyard on 22 October, with speeches in English and French and a performance by the choir.

Library

2 October 2026 2 min read

Four hundred new titles, half of them in French

The library has passed 4,000 titles after a purchase funded by the book fair in June. The new stock is weighted towards French-language novels for Classes 4 to 6, which pupils had asked for by name in last year's library survey.

Parents' association elects a new committee

Seven guardians were elected at the general meeting on 12 September. The committee meets on the first Thursday of each month in the staff room, and minutes are posted at the gate.
