06 Work with us

Teaching posts open for 2027–2028.

We recruit in March for the following September, and keep a file of applications for the French section year-round.

Grace Bilingual looks for teachers who can teach well in at least one language and are willing to work in the other. A teaching diploma or a degree with a teaching qualification is required; experience in a bilingual school is an advantage but is not essential for the nursery.

Send a short covering letter, a CV and the names of two referees to hello@gbnpsf.cm, addressed to the head teacher. Applications are acknowledged within a week; we interview at the school, and every shortlisted candidate teaches a real lesson to a real class.
