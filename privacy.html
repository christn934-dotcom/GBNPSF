01 Privacy

What we do with the information you send.

Written in plain language, because a privacy notice that nobody reads protects nobody.

Enquiries and applications

When you send the contact form or an application, the details you enter are used only to answer your enquiry and to process the application. They are kept by the school office and are not sold, rented or shared with advertisers.

In the current build these forms run in preview mode: nothing leaves your browser because no endpoint has been connected yet. Once the school connects a form endpoint, this page will be updated with where the data is stored and how long it is kept.

Photographs of pupils

Photographs of children appear on this website and on the school's social pages only where a guardian has given written permission. Permission can be withdrawn at any time by writing to the head teacher, and the image will be removed within ten working days.

Portal accounts

Student, parent and teacher portals are reserved in this build and hold no personal data. When they go live, access will be authenticated, each account will see only its own records, and passwords will be stored hashed by the server — never in the browser.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no advertising cookies and loads no third-party analytics. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is visible to that service; if the school prefers, the fonts can be hosted locally with no change to the design.

Your rights

You may ask what information the school holds about you or your child, request a correction, or ask for it to be deleted where the law allows. Write to hello@gbnpsf.cm or to the school office at Avenue des Bamouns, Quartier Njitapon, Foumban.

Last reviewed November 2026. This page is a starting point and should be checked against Cameroon's data protection requirements before the forms go live.
