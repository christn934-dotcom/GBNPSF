02 Our history

Twenty-two years on one street.

In September 2004, a group of parents in Njitapon asked a simple question: could their children learn in English and French without leaving Foumban? The answer started as two nursery classes in a rented hall on Avenue des Bamouns.

Growth was deliberately slow. A class was added only when there were teachers to staff it and room to teach it properly. That patience is why the school now runs from Nursery 1 to Class 6 on a single campus, with a waiting list rather than empty desks.

The traditions that survived are the cheap ones: assembly every morning, a reading partner from a different class, and a head teacher who still learns every new pupil's name within a fortnight.

In numbers. 480 pupils, 18 classes, 32 teachers, 4,000 library titles, and 22 graduating cohorts since 2008.

03 Mission & vision

Why the school exists, in three sentences.

Written by the founding committee in 2004, revised once in 2019, and still short enough for a child to memorise.

Mission

Teach two languages, form one person

To give every child in Foumban a rigorous bilingual primary education, in a school small enough to notice when something is wrong and structured enough to fix it.

Vision

A generation fluent in both worlds

Pupils who move into secondary school — and later into work — able to read, write and think in English and French, and to serve the community they come from.

Promise

No child quietly left behind

Progress is reviewed every half-term with guardians. If a pupil is struggling, the plan is written down, shared, and checked again in six weeks.

04 Core values

Four words we use often enough to mean them.

Values are not a poster. Each one appears in assembly, in the behaviour policy, and in the end-of-term character report.

i

Grace

Courtesy under pressure. Children are taught to greet, to wait their turn, and to disagree without cruelty.

ii

Excellence

Doing the ordinary thing well: handwriting, times tables, showing your working. Excellence is a habit before it is a prize.

iii

Service

Every class gives something back each term — a clean-up, a visit to an elder, a reading hour for the younger years.

iv

Belonging

Foumban is not a backdrop to the curriculum. Bamoun history, language and craft appear in lessons, and families are welcome at the gate.

05 A welcome

“Bring us a child who is curious. We will do the rest.”

Choosing a school is an act of trust, and I do not take it lightly. Parents ask me three things: will my child be safe, will they be taught well, and will they still be themselves at the end of it.

The answer to the first is our gate, our register and our staff. To the second, it is small classes and teachers who plan together. To the third, it is a school that teaches in two languages without asking a child to abandon the one spoken at home.

Come and see a lesson. Ask the children what they are reading. If the school is right for your family, you will know by the end of the morning.

Mrs Grace Nkeng
Head teacher since 2011

06 Facilities

Modest buildings, used properly.

We would rather describe what is in each room than how it looks on a brochure. Every space below is in daily use by pupils.

Eighteen classrooms

Thirty desks maximum, a teacher's desk, storage, and a reading corner in the nursery and lower primary rooms.

The library

4,000 titles across English and French, open at break and after school, staffed by a trained librarian.

Science room

Opened in 2026 above the library: thirty places, running water, a fume hood and kits for physics and chemistry.

Computer corner

Twelve machines on a rota for Classes 4 to 6, used for typing, research and introductory coding.

Nursery playground

Shaded by two mango trees, with soft ground, climbing frames and a sand pit reserved for the under-sixes.

Assembly hall and canteen

Morning assembly for the whole school, and a hot lunch service for 220 pupils each day.
