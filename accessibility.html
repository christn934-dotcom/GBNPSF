01 Accessibility

A school website every parent can use.

Keyboard navigation, readable contrast, honest alt text and no motion you did not ask for.

What is in place

  • A skip link to the main content on every page.
  • Full keyboard operation, including the menu, the portal list, the gallery and the calendar.
  • Visible focus outlines on links, buttons and form fields.
  • Text and background colours chosen to meet WCAG AA contrast for body copy.
  • Animation reduced or removed automatically when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Decorative graphics hidden from screen readers; images and upload slots carry text labels.
  • Forms with real labels, required-field markers and clear validation messages.

Known limitations

Image placeholders are labelled slots, not photographs, so there is no alt text to write yet — when you replace them with real images, describe what the photograph shows rather than repeating the caption.

The interactive calendar is operable by keyboard, but a static list of dates is also provided on the news and academics pages so the information is never only inside a widget.

Tell us what is broken

If something on this site is hard to use with a screen reader, a keyboard, or a slow connection, please write to hello@gbnpsf.cm or call the office. We would rather fix it than be told it meets a standard.
