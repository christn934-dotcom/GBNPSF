02 Admission requirements

What to bring, and when.

Applications are accepted throughout the year, but places are confirmed in the order applications are completed. A completed application means the form, the documents and the registration fee — not the form alone.

Documents

  • Birth certificate or a certified attestation of birth.
  • Two recent passport photographs of the child.
  • Previous school report and transfer certificate (Classes 2–6).
  • Copy of the guardian's national identity card.
  • Vaccination record, requested at registration.

03 Application process

Five steps, about three weeks.

Nothing here requires a broker or an intermediary. If anyone asks for a payment to secure a place, please tell the head teacher directly.

  • Step 1

    Enquire or visit

    Call the office or come for a Tuesday or Thursday tour. You will meet a class teacher, not only the administrator.

  • Step 2

    Submit the application

    Complete the form below or collect a paper copy at the gate. Attach the documents listed above.

  • Step 3

    Meet the class teacher

    A short play session for nursery applicants, or a reading and mathematics assessment for Classes 1–6.

  • Step 4

    Receive an offer

    We write within five working days of the assessment, with the class, the language section and the fee statement.

  • Step 5

    Register and pay

    Return the signed acceptance with the registration fee within ten days to hold the place. Uniforms are collected in August.

The office keeps a waiting list by class and by language section. Being on it does not require a payment.

05 Questions

What guardians ask us most.

If your question is not here, call the admissions line on +237 6 90 00 00 01 between 08:00 and 16:00.

At what age can my child join Nursery 1?

Children join Nursery 1 in the September of the year they turn three, and Nursery 2 the following year. A child who turns three between September and December may be admitted in January if the class has space and the head teacher agrees.

Does my child need to speak English or French before joining?

No. Children are admitted speaking one language, both, or neither as a school language. Language support classes run twice a week in the first year, in groups of six, at no extra cost.

What documents are required?

A birth certificate or attestation, two passport photographs, the previous school report and transfer certificate where applicable, and a copy of the guardian's identity card. The vaccination record is requested at registration rather than at application.

How much are the school fees?

Registration is 15,000 FCFA, payable once. Nursery tuition is 45,000 FCFA per term and primary tuition 55,000 FCFA per term. Siblings receive a 10% reduction on the second child and 15% on the third. The Class 6 examination fee is billed separately in February.

Can fees be paid in instalments?

Yes. Tuition may be paid in two instalments per term, with the second due before the fourth week. Families with three or more children may request a monthly plan from the bursar.

Is there an entrance test?

Nursery applicants meet the class teacher for a short play-based session. Applicants to Classes 1 to 6 sit a short reading and mathematics assessment so that we place them accurately. It is a placement exercise, not a competitive examination.

Do you provide transport?

Not at present. The school is within walking distance of Njitapon, the palace quarter and the central market, and many families share the school run. Ask the office — we can usually introduce you to parents on the same route.
