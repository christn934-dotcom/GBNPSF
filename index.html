01 The school

Small enough to notice, serious enough to prepare.

Grace Bilingual opened in 2004 with two nursery classes and a borrowed hall. Twenty-two years later we teach 480 children on one campus in Njitapon, and we still keep class sizes under thirty so a teacher can hear every reading voice.

Children arrive speaking one language and leave writing two — without losing the one they dream in. School charter, 2004
  • English and French taught daily from Nursery 1, by subject specialists.
  • Continuous assessment alongside end-of-term examinations.
  • Every child's progress reviewed with guardians twice a term.

03 Programmes

One school, four stages of growing up.

Children move through the school in steps that are planned together, so the handover between classes is a conversation rather than a surprise.

01

Nursery

Ages 3–5

Nursery 1 and 2: language through play, storytelling in both languages, first numbers, music and outdoor discovery.

 02

Primary

Ages 6–11

Classes 1 to 6 following the national curriculum in English and French, with reading, mathematics and science at the core.

 03

Secondary

Ages 12–15

Form 1 to Form 4 in preparation: subject teaching, laboratory work and the study habits a lycée expects.

 04

Advanced studies

Ages 16–18

A-level and baccalauréat preparation for our graduates: revision seminars, mock examinations and university guidance.

Secondary and advanced studies open progressively; ask the office which years are running this term.
