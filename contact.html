04 Finding the campus

Five minutes from the palace.

From the central market, take Avenue des Bamouns towards the palace and turn right after the Njitapon water point. The school is the walled compound with the green gate and the mango trees, opposite the small carpentry workshop.

  • By foot: 12 minutes from the market, 8 from the palace.
  • By moto-taxi: ask for “Grace Bilingual, Njitapon” — drivers know the gate.
  • By car: parking for eight vehicles inside the compound; more on the street.
  • Accessibility: the office entrance is step-free; tell us in advance if a lift is needed.
